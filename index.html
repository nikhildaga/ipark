Got Questions?

FAQ

1. What is iPark?
iPark is a digital parking management platform designed to simplify vehicle parking operations for users, valet partners, and property owners through a mobile application.
2. Who can use the iPark app?
Vehicle owners, guests visiting supported locations, authorized valet staff, and parking administrators. Access is linked to supported parking facilities.
3. Do I need to create an account?
Yes. Certain features require account creation or logging in to ensure secure access and accurate parking records.
4. What data does iPark collect?
We collect only necessary data including name, contact details, vehicle number, parking timestamps, and diagnostic device information.
5. Is my personal data secure?
Yes. We use industry-standard security protocols, secure servers, and restricted access controls to ensure your data is safe.
6. Does the app use location services?
Location services may be used to enable specific parking features, but always operate strictly with user permission and device settings.
