About Us

Who We Are

iPark (ParkoBots Private Limited) is a next-generation technology startup focused on solving real-world parking problems through automation, robotics, and seamless digital integration. Our intelligent parking assistant empowers property owners and hospitality businesses to manage vehicle flow, valet operations, and customer communication efficiently via WhatsApp API and smart QR codes.

Our Mission

To revolutionize urban parking by offering secure, space-saving, and fully automated solutions that enhance user convenience and operational control.

Our Vision

To become India’s leading provider of valet automation and digital parking management solutions, expanding across smart cities and hospitality hubs.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is iPark?

iPark is a digital parking management platform designed to simplify vehicle parking operations for users, valet partners, and property owners through a mobile application.

2. Who can use the iPark app?

The iPark app can be used by:

  • Vehicle owners and guests using supported parking locations
  • Authorized valet staff
  • Parking administrators and partners

Access is provided only for approved and supported parking facilities.

3. Do I need to create an account to use iPark?

Yes. Certain features of the iPark app require users to create an account or log in using verified credentials to ensure secure access and accurate parking records.

4. What data does iPark collect?

iPark collects only the data necessary for core functionality, which may include:

  • Name and contact details
  • Vehicle number
  • Parking activity and timestamps
  • Device information for app performance and security

All data collection is governed by our Privacy Policy.

5. How is my data used?

User data is used strictly for:

  • Managing parking operations
  • Sending parking confirmations and updates
  • Improving app performance and security

iPark does not sell user data to third parties.

6. Is my personal data secure?

Yes. iPark uses industry-standard security measures to protect user data, including secure servers and restricted access controls.

7. Does iPark share data with third parties?

Data is shared only with:

  • Authorized parking partners
  • Service providers required for app functionality (such as messaging or hosting services)

All partners are bound by confidentiality and data protection agreements.

8. Does the iPark app use location services?

Location services may be used only when required to enable parking-related features. Location access is always subject to user permission and device settings.

9. Does iPark send notifications?

Yes. iPark may send:

  • Parking confirmation messages
  • Status updates
  • Service-related notifications

Users can manage notification preferences from their device settings.

10. Is iPark free to use?

The iPark app is free to download. Certain services may involve parking or service fees as defined by the parking facility or partner.

11. Does iPark support in-app payments?

Payment features, if enabled, are processed securely through authorized payment gateways and comply with applicable regulations.

12. Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Users may request account deletion and data removal by contacting us at the support details provided below, in accordance with applicable laws.
